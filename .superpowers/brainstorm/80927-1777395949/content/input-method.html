How should users submit news for analysis?

This determines the main interaction on the home page

FakeBuster — Paste Text
Paste the full article text or headline here...

A — Paste Text

User pastes the article body or headline directly. Works for any language, any source, even screenshots OCR'd by the user.

FakeBuster — Submit URL

We'll fetch and analyze the article for you

B — URL Input

User pastes a link; the app fetches and parses the article. Convenient, but CORS and paywalls can block fetching. Needs a backend proxy.

FakeBuster — Both
Paste article or headline text here...

C — Both (Tabs)

Tabbed interface: paste text OR enter URL. Most flexible for users, slightly more complex to build.
